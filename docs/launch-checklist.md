# Public Launch Checklist

- Production URL uses HTTPS.
- News search, category filters, and article pages load current headlines.
- Article pages show original headline, simplified summary, ASL gloss, and sign sequence.
- Missing sign clips are visible to admins.
- Feedback forms submit incorrect gloss, incorrect sign, ratings, and general feedback.
- Admin dashboard shows analytics, failures, feedback, ratings, and bug reports.
- Accessibility statement, privacy policy, terms, contact, FAQ, and about pages are published.
- Backup restore procedure has been tested against a staging database.
- Monitoring alerts are configured for backend health, error rate, latency, and failed sign sequence jobs.
- Experimental avatar work is isolated from production routes and navigation.
